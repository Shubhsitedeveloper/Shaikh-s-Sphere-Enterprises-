    A bedroom that stays quiet all night.

  3. Living room: split AC

    Air that sweeps the room, not the sofa.

    Placed so the air sweeps the length of the room instead of blowing straight at the sofa. The outdoor unit goes on the terrace, out of sight and earshot.

  4. Office: ducted cooling

    One concealed unit, even air at every desk.

    A concealed unit above the false ceiling feeds insulated GI ducts. Diffusers spread cool air evenly across the desks, and warm air returns through a ceiling grille.

  5. Restaurant: cassette units

    No table sits in a draught.

    Ceiling cassettes throw air outwards along the ceiling so no table sits in a draught. The dining hall is kept at slightly higher pressure so kitchen smells stay in the kitchen.

  6. Kitchen: exhaust and fresh air

    Heat and fumes go straight up and out.

    The hood captures heat and fumes over the range. A duct carries them up the service shaft to the terrace, and fresh air is brought in to replace what is removed.

How do I know what size AC I need?

Size depends on floor area, ceiling height, sunlight, glass, the number of people and the equipment in the room. The estimator above gives a first figure. For the final answer we do a heat-load calculation during a free site visit.

What is the difference between VRF and ductable AC?

A ductable unit cools one large zone through ducts from a single indoor unit. A VRF system connects many indoor units to one outdoor system and lets every room set its own temperature, so it saves power when only part of the building is in use.

How often should an AC be serviced?

For most homes, a service before summer and another after the monsoon works well. Offices, shops and restaurants that run their units all day usually need a visit every three months.

Do you take up only installation, or supply as well?

Both. We can supply and install the complete system, or install equipment you have already bought. We will tell you honestly if the equipment does not suit the space.

Do you handle emergency breakdowns?

Yes. Call or WhatsApp us with the problem and your location. AMC customers get priority response.
